### Project Overview

This project analyses company and industry performance through revenue, workforce size, sector distribution and company registration status. The interactive report helps users compare business performance across industries, companies and time periods.

### Business Questions

- Which industries and sectors generate the highest revenue?
- Which companies contribute the most revenue?
- How are companies distributed by registration status?
- How does revenue vary across weekdays and years?
- What is the employee footprint represented in the dataset?

### Tools

- Power BI — report development, data visualisation, slicers and interactive analysis.
- DAX — measures for revenue, company counts, active-company revenue and employee metrics.

### Dashboard Pages

#### 1. Company & Industry Overview

Presents headline KPIs for total employees, total revenue, revenue from active companies and total industries. Supporting visuals compare revenue by industry, revenue by active company and industry distribution by sector. Year and weekday slicers enable focused analysis.

#### 2. Revenue & Company Analysis

Explores revenue by sector, company registration status, leading companies by revenue and revenue by weekday. Year and industry slicers allow users to interactively filter the results.

### Analysis Approach

1. Organise company, industry, sector, registration-status and time information for analysis.
2. Use DAX measures to summarise revenue, company counts and employee metrics.
3. Compare performance across industries, sectors, companies, registration status and weekdays.
4. Use slicers to explore how results change under different filters.
5. Interpret the results in a business context and identify areas for further investigation.

### Key Findings

- Revenue contribution varies across industries and sectors, highlighting differences in business performance.
- A small number of companies account for a significant share of the revenue generated.
- Active, inactive and suspended companies represent different proportions of the companies analysed.
- Employee distribution provides insight into workforce size across the companies.
- Revenue patterns vary across weekdays, offering useful insights into business activity.

### Business Recommendations

- Review the industries and companies that contribute the largest share of revenue to understand the drivers of their performance.
- Investigate inactive and suspended companies to understand their status and potential business implications.
- Compare weekday revenue patterns when planning operational activity and resource allocation.
- Monitor employee counts alongside revenue to support workforce and performance discussions.

### Limitations

- The analysis is based on the data available in the dataset and may not reflect the complete performance of all companies or industries.
- The findings are limited to the variables provided and do not account for external factors that may influence company revenue and performance.
- The analysis identifies trends and patterns but does not establish causal relationships.
